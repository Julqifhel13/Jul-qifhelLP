## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, polished digital presence tailored for an elite executive Virtual Assistant and Automation/GoHighLevel Specialist. It merges enterprise-grade operational rigor with modern digital automation capability.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Architectural & Systematic:** Conveys systematic thinking, organized workflows, and technical mastery. The UI feels calculated, dependable, and efficient.
- **High-Velocity Executive Partner:** Appeals to high-ticket founders, marketing agency operators, and enterprise executives who need rapid, friction-free implementation without handholding.
- **Electric Precision:** Strikes a balance between a commanding slate dark mode and razor-sharp electric accents (cyan and royal indigo), signaling advanced automation and technical depth.

### Visual Style
A tailored hybrid of **SaaS-Minimalism** and **Subtle Dark Glassmorphism**. The system leverages deep obsidian and slate foundations, structured architectural borders with calibrated 1px hair lines, and high-precision micro-surfaces. Elevation relies on subtle chromatic halos and backdrop filtration rather than muddy drop shadows, ensuring credential badges, metrics, and workflow timelines read with supreme clarity.

## Colors

The palette establishes high technical authority through a calibrated dark mode hierarchy built on deep blue-slate values rather than lifeless true-black.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#4F46E5` - Electric Indigo):** Represents logic, high-tier technical architecture, and primary conversion pathways. Utilized for high-intent actions, primary interactive states, and glowing brand focal points.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` - Cyber Cyan):** Represents connectivity, API data transfer, and automated speed. Used as a high-visibility accent on badges, metrics, active states, and interactive timeline nodes.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Emerald Automation):** Represents active system status, verified integrations, pipeline success metrics, and "Available for Hire" indicators.
- **Neutral Base (`#0B0F19` - Deep Obsidian Slate):** Provides depth and extreme contrast while remaining easy on executive eyes during long review sessions.

### Semantic Tonal Application
- **Surface Canvas (Layer 0):** `#070A11`
- **Surface Container Low (Layer 1):** `#0D1322` (Card backplates, timeline track)
- **Surface Container Mid (Layer 2):** `#151E34` (Hover states, badge backings, inputs)
- **Surface Container High (Layer 3):** `#1E2B4A` (Modal overlays, floating navigation panels)
- **Border / Outline Tone:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` baseline, escalating to `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.35)` upon hover or active system selection.
- **Text Hierarchies:**
  - **Text-Primary:** `#F8FAFC` (100% white-slate)
  - **Text-Secondary:** `#94A3B8` (Cool muted slate for metadata and descriptions)
  - **Text-Tertiary:** `#64748B` (Sub-captions, timestamps, inactive icons)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy employs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for both display headlines and structural body copy to generate a razor-sharp, modern executive presence with clean geometric apertures. For technical metrics, platform stack integration pills, and automation status indicators, **JetBrains Mono** is layered in as the dedicated label font.

### Styling Directives
- **Hero & Section Titles:** Emphasize high contrast using tight letter spacing (`-0.03em` on display sizes). Headings should frequently combine solid white-slate text with selective cyan-to-indigo gradient fills for focal keywords (e.g., "High-ROI Automations").
- **Body & Readability:** Keep executive bio summaries and case study copy set at a relaxed 1.6x - 1.66x line height to prevent fatigue.
- **Monospaced Micro-copy:** Use `label-code` for API webhooks, GoHighLevel pipeline tags, and metric deltas (e.g., `+340% Efficiency`).

## Layout & Spacing

The portfolio is architected on a **12-column responsive fluid grid** pinned within a centralized `1200px` max-width container, surrounded by defensive side gutters.

### Form Factor Adaptations
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns with `2rem` (32px) gutters. Hero utilizes a 7/5 split (Executive bio & CTA stack on the left; real-time interactive automation pipeline preview card on the right). Timelines leverage an alternating dual-column layout with a central glowing trace line.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Cards reflow to 2 columns. Specialized metrics collapse into a 2x2 grid.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters. Full-width linear stack. The timeline switches to a left-anchored rail with nodes tracing down the margin. Consultation booking controls stick to a fixed bottom dock with backdrop glassmorphism.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks adhere to an 8px grid foundation, spacing out at `space-4xl` (96px) on desktop and `space-2xl` (48px) on mobile screens to preserve high-status negative space.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy in this system avoids thick drop shadows in favor of **ambient luminescent layers**, crisp border strokes, and backdrop blurs.

### Surface Tiers
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#070A11` background.
- **Layer 1 (Modular Surface):** Tinted `#0D1322` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. Used for passive cards, credential shells, and tech stack lists.
- **Layer 2 (Elevated Active Card / Timeline Node):** Glass-backed `#151E34` at 85% opacity with `16px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). Encased with a subtle directional gradient border: top-left `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)` fading to bottom-right `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.1)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Primary Consultation Docks):** Multi-layered depth. Ambient glow cast from behind the card: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.15), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.

### Chromatic Glow Accents
Interactive interactive points utilize subtle radial gradient glows positioned absolute behind elements (`pointer-events: none; opacity: 0.15; filter: blur(60px); background: radial-gradient(circle, #4F46E5 0%, transparent 70%)`).

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp technological precision with human approachability by using **roundedness level 2 (0.5rem standard radius)**.

### Curvature Taxonomy
- **Micro UI Elements (Pills, Status dots, Badges):** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for credential certification badges, status indicators, and chip elements.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Text inputs):** Standard `0.5rem` (8px) radius, providing clean structural definition without feeling bubbly or toy-like.
- **Content Cards & Timeline Nodes:** `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px), giving ample boundary for internal padding without encroaching on content density.
- **Feature Modals & Hero Canvas Banners:** `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for expansive macro surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Consultation CTA:** Electric indigo-to-cyan gradient background (`linear-gradient(135deg, #4F46E5 0%, #06B6D4 100%)`), pure white typography (`button-text`), `0.5rem` radius, with subtle cyan inner specular border (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.3)`). Hover triggers an ambient cyan glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.4)`) and a `transform: translateY(-1px)` lift.
- **Secondary Action (View Automations / Resume):** Transparent core, `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text in `#F8FAFC`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` and border transitions to `#06B6D4`.
- **Icon Buttons:** Fixed 40x40px or 48x48px squares, `0.5rem` radius, containing crisp SVG icons (LinkedIn, GitHub, Calendar, Mail).

### Credential Badges & Platform Chips
- Pill-shaped (`9999px`) containers.
- Background: `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08)` with a `1px` border of `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)`.
- Content: Micro platform logo (e.g., GoHighLevel, Zapier, Make, Notion), accompanied by bold `label-badge` text in `#06B6D4` or `#A5F3FC`. Include an optional tertiary emerald pulse dot to represent "Certified" or "Active".

### Interactive Timeline Cards (Career & Automation Case Studies)
- Built with a vertical progressive connection rail: a 2px vertical stroke in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` with active nodes illuminated in `#06B6D4`.
- Card surface: `Layer 1` dark slate surface, padding `1.5rem`, `1rem` radius.
- Metadata row: Contains role title, client enterprise category, and duration formatted in `label-code`.
- Expansion detail: Accordion or tabbed view showcasing "Challenge vs. Automated Architecture vs. Measurable ROI" (e.g., "Saved 22 hrs/week, $45k pipeline processed").

### Inputs & Booking Form Controls
- Height: 48px. 
- Background: `#0B0F19`, `1px` border in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Radius `0.5rem`.
- Text: `#F8FAFC` with placeholder text in `#64748B`.
- Focus State: Border snaps to `#4F46E5`, paired with an electric indigo focus ring: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.2)`.

### High-Conversion Client Consultation CTA Dock
- An anchor-pinned card or persistent bottom banner.
- Features real-time availability indicator: pulsating green badge (`#10B981`) reading "Booking Q2 Client Automations".
- Direct integration trigger with embedded scheduler (Cal.com / Calendly modal trigger) alongside time-zone synchronization tags.
